About FlowReach

Bridging the gap between generosity and need

FlowReach is building the digital infrastructure to connect menstrual care donations with communities that need them most — eliminating duplication, delay, and uncertainty.

Our Mission

To create a transparent, efficient coordination system that ensures menstrual care donations reach the right communities at the right time — without duplication, waste, or delay.

We believe that access to menstrual care should never be a barrier to school attendance, health, or dignity. By connecting willing donors with verified needs through data-driven matching, we're removing the friction that keeps critical products from reaching the communities that need them most.

Our Vision

A future where every girl, woman, and menstruating person in underserved communities has reliable access to menstrual products — where coordination between donors and communities is seamless, transparent, and equitable.

We envision FlowReach evolving from a rules-based matching engine into a machine-learning platform that learns from real delivery outcomes, continuously optimizing how donations flow to where they're needed most. At scale, this system can serve as a model for coordinating any essential health product to underserved populations.

Core Values

Transparency

Every decision is visible. Our matching rules are open-source and explained. Communities see why they were matched or prioritized.

Equity

We prioritize the communities with the greatest need. No product goes to duplicate locations while others remain unmet.

Dignity

Menstruation is not a shame. Our design centers the voices of those we serve, respecting autonomy and choice.

Efficiency

We eliminate waste — whether duplication, delay, or misalignment. Every step is optimized for speed and impact.

Data-Driven

Decisions are grounded in verified need, not assumptions. We measure outcomes and adapt based on real-world delivery evidence.

Collaboration

We exist to serve communities and amplify NGOs. Our tools are built with, not for, the organizations working on the ground.
