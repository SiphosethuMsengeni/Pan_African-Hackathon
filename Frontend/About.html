About Us

Admin-Controlled NGO Verification & Delivery

FlowReach ensures every NGO is verified and every delivery is tracked with full accountability.

FlowReach is a complete coordination platform with admin-controlled NGO verification and delivery management.

Admin Responsibilities:
  • Verify NGOs: Check NPO registration, DSD registry, and supporting documents
  • Match Requests: Assign verified NGOs to community requests
  • Update Delivery Status: Progress requests through each stage
  • Monitor Duplicates: Detect and coordinate multiple NGOs supporting the same community
  • Admins CANNOT submit requests — only community members can
